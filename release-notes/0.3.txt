Groceries 0.3

WHAT'S NEW
• Better recipe ideas: you'll get six simple recipes with quantities, based on what's in your kitchen and what you usually buy. Each one links to a video of a similar dish.
• Favorite cuisines: pick the cuisines your household likes, and most recipe ideas will lean that way. You'll be asked once; change them anytime in Settings.
• "Tonight…": on the Home screen, choose up to three cuisines you're in the mood for and get recipes just for those. They stay until you clear them or the next day's refresh.
• Recipe ingredients leave out salt, pepper, oil, and water, so they don't end up on your shopping list.
• Delete past lists: swipe left on a past list on Home, or use the list's menu. You'll be asked to confirm first.
• The list menu now has icons.
