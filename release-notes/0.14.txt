Groceries 0.14

ADDED
• Recipe box: save your household's recipes in one place everyone can see, and add what you're missing to a list in a tap.
• Ask for a recipe: chat with AI for ideas, like "chicken thighs in the dutch oven", ask for changes, and save the ones you like.
• Paste a recipe: paste one from a website, a message, or a note. It's sorted into ingredients and steps, keeping the original wording.
• YouTube links: paste a link to a cooking video and the recipe comes from the creator's description or pinned comment.
• Share to Groceries: share a recipe or a video from another app (Claude, Gemini, YouTube, your browser) straight into Groceries.
• Servings: change how many people a recipe serves and the ingredient amounts adjust.

CHANGED
• Home: shopping lists come first, with a "Start a list" button when none is going. Past lists are tucked into one row at the bottom.
• Daily recipe ideas, favorite cuisines, and "Tonight" are gone, replaced by the recipe box.
