Groceries 0.1 – first test version

Thanks for trying this out! Groceries is a shared grocery list for your household that learns what you buy and suggests easy recipes.

GETTING STARTED
• Sign up with Google or email.
• Start a household, or join one with the 6-character invite code someone shares with you (Settings → Invite code).

WHAT'S IN THIS VERSION
• Shared lists: everyone in the household sees changes instantly, and it works offline.
• Stores: start a list for a store near you (logos for common chains), or skip and keep a general list.
• Smart suggestions: items you buy often show up as one-tap chips on new lists.
• Recipes for you: simple recipes from YouTube, based on what you buy and what's in "My kitchen". Tap "New ideas" for a fresh batch.
• My kitchen: add what you already have (freezer, meat delivery, pantry) so recipes use it up first.
• Add a recipe's ingredients to a list in one tap; things already in your kitchen are left off.
• Finish & close out a list when you're done shopping; unchecked items can roll over to a new list. Past lists can be reopened.
• Settings: rename the household, invite people, and manage members.

GOOD TO KNOW
• The app asks for your location the first time you start a list, to show nearby stores. It's optional, and only your approximate area is used.
• Recipe ideas appear once you've added a few items to lists, or right away if you add things to My kitchen.
• New recipe ideas are limited to a handful per day.

WHAT TO TRY
• Invite someone in your household and add items at the same time.
• Start a list from the store picker and do a real shopping trip with it.
• Add a recipe's ingredients to a list.
