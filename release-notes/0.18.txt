Groceries 0.18

ADDED
• Notes on list items: press and hold an item and choose Add a note ("The low-sodium one"). Everyone in the household sees it under the item, and it goes along when you share the list.
• Pictures on recipe cards: recipes from YouTube show the video's thumbnail, and the rest get a food emoji picked from the name.
• Recipe search on Home: tap the search bar above your recipes and start typing.

CHANGED
• Recipes on Home are now cards, two across, and you scroll down through all of them.
• Better recipe search: the closest matches come first, plurals and accents don't matter, and it also looks in descriptions and notes. When an ingredient matched, the card says which one.
• New list moved up next to the Shopping lists title.
• Pressing and holding a list item opens a small menu, where you can add a note or edit the name.

FIXED
• Long ingredient amounts no longer squeeze the ingredient's name into a sliver.
