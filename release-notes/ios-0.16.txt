Groceries 0.16

ADDED
• Share: send a recipe or what's left on a shopping list to anyone, from the share button at the top of the screen.
• Siri and Shortcuts: say "Add to my Groceries list", "What's on my Groceries list", "Start a Groceries list", "Check something off in Groceries" or "Open a recipe in Groceries". The same actions are in the Shortcuts app.
• Bigger screens: on a large screen (like an opened foldable), Home shows your lists and recipes side by side, and a recipe shows the ingredients next to the steps. Other screens stay a comfortable width instead of stretching edge to edge.
