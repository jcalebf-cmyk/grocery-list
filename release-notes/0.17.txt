Groceries 0.17

ADDED
• More than one household: join or start another from Settings, under Other households, without a second account. Each one has its own lists, stores and recipes. Once you're in two, tap the name at the top of Home to switch.
• Delete a household: if you're the only one in it and you have another, you can delete it in Settings.
• Sign in with Apple: it opens Apple's page in your browser. If you use Apple on an iPhone, it's the same account.

CHANGED
• New passwords need at least 8 characters. Your current password still works.
• Deleting your account now covers every household you're in, and asks who takes over each one you own.

FIXED
• Signing in on a new phone no longer flashes the household setup screen.
• If the app can't connect when it opens, it says so and lets you sign out.
