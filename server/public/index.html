Groceries

The shared grocery list for your household

Everyone in your home adds to the same list, from iPhone or Android, and sees changes as they happen.

  • Smart suggestionsThe things you buy often, ready to add in a tap.
  • Nearby storesKeep a list for each store you shop at.
  • Recipe ideasIdeas built around what your household buys, leaning toward the cuisines you like.
